# Record Luis’s side

Record the four witness replies below. The agent’s questions stay synthetic. Play Luis as a concerned husband concentrating on the questions: natural, steady, slightly distracted, with more certainty after checking the phone. Use your own voice.

Save the selected take of each clip in this folder. WAV is ideal; M4A from Voice Memos is also fine. Use the filename stem below with the matching extension.

| Filename | Agent cue (do not record) | Your recorded line | Delivery |

| luis-01-memory.wav | When did you last personally speak with Rosa when she seemed her usual self? | This morning, around eight-thirty. | Recall the conversation. A small hesitation before “around” is natural; the time is an estimate. |
| luis-02-call-log.wav | Can you check your recent calls for the exact time? | One second. It was 8:32 AM. | Pause after “One second” as if checking the phone, then read the time with certainty. Say “eight thirty-two A.M.” |

Is that correct? | Yes, that's correct. | A simple, assured answer after listening. No dramatic emphasis. |

Record two takes of each and choose the most conversational. Keep the exact words, but use natural pauses and breathing. Leave about one second of quiet at both ends.

Use a quiet room and keep the same microphone distance across clips. A phone or laptop mic is enough. If listening to the demo for context, use headphones so the agent’s audio does not bleed into the recording. Leave music, filters, and effects off.

Do not match the existing clip durations. Captions, chapters, and the evidence update should be retimed to the selected recordings during integration.
