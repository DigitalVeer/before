# Showing Before

Open https://before.veersingh.com/demo and start with sound. The opening establishes the missing history; the interview and chart then become the two focuses. The recorded conversation lasts about a minute.

The story: Rosa cannot speak. The people who know when she was last at her usual baseline are somewhere else. A voice agent asks Luis what he personally observed, checks his call log, and reads the whole claim back. The chart changes only after his yes. Four other agents are then revealed gathering independent accounts for the same patient. Five source-linked accounts leave the doctor with a fuller history to review.

If presenting aloud, keep it to three lines:

- Before playback: “Rosa can’t tell the doctor what happened. Her family and the people around her can.”
- At confirmation: “The exact time comes from the witness, and the claim waits for his yes.”
- At the reveal: “Luis wasn’t the only call. Each account keeps its source, and the doctor decides what it means.”

Click a source in the ending to show its quote, time anchor, read-back confirmation, and transcript. Chapters let you return to the anchor, confirmation, or reveal. Restart returns to the premise.

## Clinical premise

Stroke can cause sudden difficulty speaking, and fast treatment can reduce brain damage. These facts support the opening’s urgency. They do not establish that this product reduces mortality, saves treatment time, or determines treatment eligibility.

Sources: [CDC: stroke signs and symptoms](https://www.cdc.gov/stroke/signs-symptoms/index.html), [NINDS: assess and treat](https://www.ninds.nih.gov/health-information/stroke/assess-and-treat).

The 190, 38, and 22 minute values are synthetic intervals between reported baseline and symptom discovery. They are not minutes saved. All cases are synthetic; audio is prerecorded. The parallel calls are a scripted concept, with separate transcripts checked by the same validator. The guided demo makes no chart writes.
